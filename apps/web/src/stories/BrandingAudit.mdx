## What is already consistent

- All panel / mission-row / terminal / brand-bar colors go through `var(--…)` tokens — the only hard-coded colour
  literal is the Cesium globe colour (WebGL can't read CSS) and `app/layout.tsx` `themeColor`.
- Phosphor literal discipline holds (`scripts/lint-phosphor.sh` passes): the OKLCH phosphor value only appears in
  `tokens.css` and `trust-gradient.ts`.
- `.trust-readout` tabular numerics are applied to every score numeral (TrustReadout, CandidateCards).
- Surface tiers are used as specified: base → panel → elevated.

## Components without a story of their own

- **`RootLayout`** (`app/layout.tsx`) — renders `<html>`/`<body>` and Next `<Script>` tags, so it cannot mount
  inside a story. Its effects (global.css, Cesium base URL + script) are reproduced by `.storybook/preview.tsx` and
  the Cesium story loader.
- **`CandidateCard`, `CitationHover`** (private in `CandidateCards.tsx`) — not exported; covered by
  Panel/CandidateCards (all branches, including the empty filler and no-munitions states).
- **`AoeCardBlock`** (`panel/AoeCardBlock.tsx`) — rendered by CandidateCards; covered by the AoE stories in
  COP/CesiumSpine, COP/MapSpine and Pages/COP.
- **`TraceBullets`** (private in `TrustPanel.tsx`) — covered by Panel/TrustPanel (0–3 bullets via Playground).
- **`TssMinIndicator`** (private in `TrustReadout.tsx`) — covered by Panel/TrustReadout (above / on / below the TSS minimum).
- **`RiskForm`, `Branch`, `Chip`** (private in `MissionRow.tsx`) — covered by Fires/Mission Row.
- **`AdminPanel`** (`admin/AdminPanel.tsx`) — opened by Admin/AdminMenu › Open.
- **`SpineOverlay`, `AoeKey`, `BasemapAttribution`** (`cop/`) — drawn by the spines; covered by COP/CesiumSpine and
  COP/MapSpine.
- **`SpineLoader`** (private in `Spine.tsx`) — only visible while the dynamic chunk loads; flashes in COP/Spine.
- **`SpineWithProps`** (private in `app/page.tsx`) — page-local adapter; covered by Pages/COP.
- **WebGL renderers** (`CesiumSpine`, `MapSpine`) — storied and render offline, but need a WebGL-capable browser;
  headless/CI snapshot runners without GPU will show an empty canvas.

## Dependency impact (NFR-07)

Storybook adds **six devDependencies** to `apps/web` (`storybook`, `@storybook/nextjs`, `@storybook/react`,
`@storybook/addon-essentials`, `@storybook/blocks`, `@storybook/test`, all pinned `8.6.18`). **Zero runtime
dependencies** — nothing is imported from app code, and `next build` output is unchanged. `scripts/count-deps.sh`
counts dev + runtime deps together; the ≤25 *runtime* budget is unaffected.
